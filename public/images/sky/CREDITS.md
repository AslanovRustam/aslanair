# Night sky images

Encoded from the originals by `tools/encode-sky.mjs`.

## stars.webp

- Image: "The Milky Way panorama" (eso0932a), a 360° equirectangular panorama of the night sky in galactic coordinates.
- Source: https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/ (publication JPEG, 4000×2000)
- Credit: ESO/S. Brunier
- Licence: CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Changes: resampled to 4096×2048 and its sky background taken to black; the backdrop tilts it over the sky and adds it to its own night gradient.

## cloud-sea.webp

- Image: marine stratocumulus over the southern Indian Ocean, Aqua MODIS, 11 March 2013 (NASA Earth Observatory image record 80661).
- Source: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/80000/80661/indianocean_amo_2013070_lrg.jpg
- Credit: NASA image courtesy Jeff Schmaltz, LANCE MODIS Rapid Response
- Licence: public domain (NASA imagery, not copyrighted in the United States)
- Changes: a 4200 px square cropped, turned grey, its levels stretched and resampled to 2048×2048; the cloud sea grades it to moonlight.
